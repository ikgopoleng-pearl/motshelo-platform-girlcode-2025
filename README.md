# Girlcode2025
Fintech girlcode hackathon

## My Contribution

I collaborated with my team in conceptualising and developing a digital Motshelo platform aimed at improving transparency and trust in informal savings groups.

My contributions focused on:
- **Dashboard Design:** Contributing to the design and presentation of group activities, member contributions and trust indicators.
- **Project Planning:** Participating in discussions around project objectives, user needs and proposed features.
- **Problem-solving:** Contributing ideas towards addressing transparency and trust challenges in informal savings groups.

Through this experience, I gained exposure to collaborative software development, user-focused design and translating real-world financial challenges into technology-driven solutions.
